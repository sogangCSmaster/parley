The short version

  • We never record or store your meetings: no audio, no video, no captions.
  • What you say is sent to Google Gemini only to be transcribed and translated, then discarded.
  • We keep your account details and how many minutes you translate, so we can bill you fairly.
  • We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. No ads, no trackers.

What we collect

Account details. Your name, email address and a one-way hash of your password, or the name and email Google shares if you sign in with Google.

Billing. Payments are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record. We receive your plan, billing status and country, never your full card number.

Usage. Meeting start and end times, the number of participants, the languages chosen and the minutes translated. We use this to apply plan limits and to keep the service running.

Technical data. IP address and browser type in short-lived server logs, used for security and abuse prevention. Logs never contain what was said.

Speech and captions

When translation is on, your browser sends your speech directly to Google’s Gemini API, which returns text. The text passes through our server to be translated and delivered to the other participants.

We use Gemini’s paid service, under which Google does not use the content to train its models. We do not store audio, transcripts or translations. Each participant can save their own captions to their device as a text file.

Video and audio

Video and audio travel between participants with WebRTC encryption. When a direct connection is not possible, they pass through our relay server, which forwards encrypted data and cannot see or record it.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We use these processors only to provide the service:

  • Google (Gemini API): speech recognition and translation
  • Paddle: payments, invoices and tax
  • Amazon Web Services: hosting
  • Resend: account emails such as sign-up verification
